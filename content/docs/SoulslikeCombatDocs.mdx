Enemy combat AI for Unreal Engine 5.6 and newer: tactical movement, scored attacks, reactions, and coordination across a group. Each system stands on its own, so you can take the movement evaluator without the action scoring, or the melee traces without either. Runs on single player, listen server and dedicated server.

New here? Start with [Getting Started](/docs/soulslike-combat/getting-started) for a working enemy, then [Step-by-Step Enemy](/docs/soulslike-combat/step-by-step-enemy) to build one properly. Already have a Character and an AI Controller you cannot reparent? [Minimal Enemy](/docs/soulslike-combat/minimal-enemy) is the six components that make one fight, with nothing inherited.

## How the pieces connect

Config drives the controller, the controller decides, the pawn holds the movesets and runs the abilities. Switch to **Full wiring** to see every component, and click one for its resolution chain.
